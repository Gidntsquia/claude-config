## Requirements
Part A — worker (planned in ~/files/worker-building; edits agents/worker.md, settings.json, the worker eval)
1. (user) A classifier denial never ends the turn: the worker records the denied command and reason in WORKER_NOTES, tries at least two other routes (a different tool, a script the plan allows, a narrower command, a flag), builds everything that does not depend on it, and only then may list it as a blocker in a final message. The pogo Tuesday case (`kill` of a stuck child the runner spawned) is the fixture: the worker must finish the stint without a human.
2. (user) No turn ends in prose with a question or offer ("Which do you want?", "Should I start?", "Tell me if you want that"): the final-message rule names these as not-final, and a check in the worker eval fails any run whose last assistant text ends that way or contains a Yes/No ask outside AskUserQuestion.
3. (user) When the only remaining work is waiting on something timed (a scrape, a long run, a criterion "that only time can settle"), the worker schedules its own relaunch with `run-agent.sh worker "<same prompt>" --at …` (same model/effort), writes the handoff section in WORKER_NOTES, and ends with the scheduled line; a turn that ends with unmet timed criteria and nothing scheduled is a FAIL in the eval. (default) Default check-in gap: the plan's stint length, else 2 h.
4. (user) `settings.json` `permissions.allow` pre-allows what unsupervised workers need and the classifier otherwise asks about: `kill`/`pkill` of processes the project started, `git tag`, `git push`, `gh release create`, `tmux send-keys` to sessions the worker created, and the WSL memory commands; each entry documented in one line in `wsl/CLAUDE.local.md` or `README`.
5. (user) Worker eval audit: `agent-eval-experiment/worker/cases/c1–c9` each judged "keeps information / drop" with the reason written in `worker/README.md`; cases that never separated variants in `results/runs.jsonl` are removed; two new cases replay the pogo failures: `c10-denied-kill` (a stuck child only a denied `kill` frees; hidden test: the stint's output grows anyway and WORKER_NOTES records the denial and the route used) and `c11-wait-only` (criteria that need an hour; hidden test: a scheduled job exists via `RUN_AGENT_HOME` and the last message carries it). Old worker.md fails both; the new one passes 3/3.
Part B — planner (planned in ~/files/planner-building; edits agents/planner.md)
6. (user) The plan carries no research for the worker: the words "worker verifies/searches/measures/confirms/decides" and "unverified … worker" are banned from PLAN.md; every fact a requirement depends on is fetched, read or run by the planner before writing and listed under Facts with source and time. A fact the planner cannot reach after trying is written as Unverified with the exact call and error, and the requirement names what to build in both outcomes.
7. (user) Research budget between round 2 and writing: up to 12 lookups or commands and 10 minutes; past the budget the rest goes under Unverified as in R6. (default) The planner may run read-only project commands (counts, versions, `ps`, a timing of an existing script) to settle a fact; it still writes no files but PLAN.md.
8. (default) Planner eval (`agent-eval-experiment/planner/`) gains a check that fails a plan containing a banned phrase from R6 and counts Unverified lines; three replayed pogo asks (ladder held-out, scrape-1500, gpu round 8) produce plans with zero banned phrases.
Part C — evaluator (planned in ~/files/evaluator-building; edits agents/evaluator.md, wsl/local-tools)
9. (user) Nothing the evaluator opens is ever visible before its question: items are created minimized or off-screen from the first frame (e.g. `Start-Process -WindowStyle Minimized`, or a window opened at a position beyond the display and moved in by `show`), never "open normally then minimize"; the whole-display capture taken every 2.5 s during steps 5–6 must show no new window; a run where one appears is a FAIL recorded in EVAL.md with the capture path. `win-app.sh` gains a `--hidden` form that does this for firefox, code and wt.

18. (user) New-pane launches: `split` (new pane in this window), `window` (new tmux window) and `in <dir>` combine, e.g. `/run-agent planner "Plan part A of ~/files/claude-config/plans/PLAN.md" in ~/files/worker-building split`; four such runs in one minute start four live sessions without replacing each other; scheduled runs accept the same words and record the target.

20. (default) The uncommitted revive rework is finished one way: either the service path (unit installed by install.sh, `--check` line, CHANGELOG entry corrected) or the committed `~/.bashrc --revive --ask` path; not both. `test.sh` passes.
Release
